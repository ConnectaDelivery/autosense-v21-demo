Ask our team ↗
What is an ignition interlock device?＋

An IID is a breath-testing device connected to a vehicle’s ignition system. Approved equipment and the requirements for its use are determined by the relevant authorities. This website provides general information, not operating or installation instructions.

Does installation restore my license?＋

Not by itself. DMV or court requirements depend on your individual record. Confirm all applicable conditions before driving; we do not guarantee eligibility, reinstatement or timelines.

What should I have ready for a conversation?＋

Know which authority issued your requirements and what questions you need answered. The team can explain how to arrange a secure review of necessary documents. Do not upload or send license numbers, court documents or case details through this general inquiry form.

Can I request an appointment online?＋

You can send an appointment inquiry. Your preferred location and availability must be confirmed by the team. Submitting an inquiry does not reserve a time. This design preview only simulates the form.

Is reduced-fee assistance available?＋

Some participants may qualify. See the California Bureau of Automotive Repair’s IID information and ask the provider about the applicable process. Eligibility and assistance are not guaranteed.
